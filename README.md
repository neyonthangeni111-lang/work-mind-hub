# WorkMind Insights

MASTER WEBSITE PROMPT — WORKPLACE PSYCHOLOGY & LABOUR RELATIONS PLATFORM

Create a modern, professional AI-powered workplace psychology and labour relations management platform called WorkMind.

 

The platform combines organisational psychology, employee relations, labour relations management, workplace communication, conflict management, meeting intelligence, and professional productivity into one intelligent workspace.

 

The application should help HR professionals, labour-relations practitioners, managers, organisational psychologists, team leaders, and workplace administrators make better decisions, communicate professionally, identify workplace issues, manage cases, and stay organised.

 

The product should feel like a combination of:

 

Organisational Psychology Platform + Labour Relations Management System + AI Professional Assistant + Workplace Productivity Dashboard.

 

Do not make it feel like a generic HR system or generic AI chatbot.

 

The AI should be integrated into meaningful workplace and people-management workflows.

18. RESPONSIBLE AI

 

This is extremely important because the application deals with people and workplace matters.

 

Display:

 

“AI-generated content may contain errors or bias. Review and verify all outputs before using them in professional, employment, disciplinary, psychological or legal contexts.”

 

Also display:

 

“Do not enter unnecessary confidential employee information, sensitive personal data, medical information, or other protected information.”

 

Add:

 

AI Usage Guidelines

 

Explain:

 

AI does not make employment decisions

AI does not diagnose psychological conditions

AI should not determine disciplinary outcomes

AI should not replace professional judgement

Users must verify generated information

Human review is required for important workplace decisions

Confidential information should be protected

25. FINAL PRODUCT FEEL

 

The final application should feel like a serious professional platform, not a school management application.

 

Think:

 

Organisational Psychology + Labour Relations + HR Case Management + Professional Productivity + Responsible AI

 

The interface should make users feel:

 

“I can understand this situation, organise the information, communicate professionally, and decide what to do next.”

 

The AI should assist professional judgement rather than replace it.

 

The most important design principle is:

 

People first. Evidence second. AI third.

 

Build the entire interface around trust, confidentiality, fairness, psychological awareness, labour-relations professionalism and practical productivity
